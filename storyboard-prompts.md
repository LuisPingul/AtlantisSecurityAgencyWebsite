# Atlantis Security Agency: Storyboard Image Prompts (6 scenes × 6 frames)

Each prompt produces **one image per scene**, laid out as a 6-panel storyboard (3 columns × 2 rows, read left→right, top→bottom). Each panel is about 1.7 seconds of the 10-second clip.

**Consistency rules (keep identical across all prompts):**
- **Officer:** a man or woman in their 30s, short dark hair, navy-blue tactical uniform, gold badge on the chest, white "ATLANTIS" patch (no readable text needed), black cap.
- **Look:** cinematic, deep navy and teal grade with gold highlights.
- **Tip:** generate each storyboard first, then use it as the reference image in Flow's frames-to-video / "ingredients" for the matching scene's video prompt.

**Storyboard Style Anchor (paste at the start of every prompt):**
> Professional film storyboard sheet, 16:9, a clean 3×2 grid of 6 equal panels separated by thin white borders, each panel a cinematic frame with consistent characters and color grade (deep navy, teal, gold accents), numbered panels 1–6 in the corner, no other text, no captions.

---

## Scene 1: The Threat (0:00–0:10)
[Storyboard Style Anchor] Six sequential panels of a tense night-time story:
1. Wide establishing shot of a rainy city skyline at night, neon reflections on wet streets.
2. Street-level shot of an empty road, puddles rippling, flickering streetlight.
3. Exterior of a dark corporate building, glass lobby doors, dim interior lights.
4. Inside the lobby, lights flickering, long shadows across marble floor.
5. A shadowy hooded figure's silhouette lingers behind a glass door.
6. Extreme close-up of the door handle slowly moving, with a cold blue light across it.

## Scene 2: The Guardian Arrives (0:10–0:20)
[Storyboard Style Anchor] Six sequential panels of a hopeful sunrise story:
1. Wide shot of the same city skyline as the sun rises, golden light spreading over buildings.
2. Light rays hit the wet street, mist glowing gold.
3. Low-angle shot of the officer's boots stepping forward in slow motion.
4. Medium shot of the officer in navy uniform walking confidently toward the camera.
5. Close-up of the gold badge catching the sunlight.
6. Hero close-up: the officer looks straight into the lens with calm confidence, golden backlight.

## Scene 3: Trained Professionals (0:20–0:30)
[Storyboard Style Anchor] Six sequential panels in a bright modern training facility:
1. Wide shot of a modern training hall with a team of uniformed officers standing in formation.
2. Instructor in front of a tactical map screen, pointing.
3. Over-the-shoulder shot of officers taking notes in a briefing.
4. Close-ups of focused, determined faces.
5. Officers practicing a calm, professional radio and communication drill.
6. Group shot: the team turns together toward the camera, standing proud.

## Scene 4: Technology & Surveillance (0:30–0:40)
[Storyboard Style Anchor] Six sequential panels in a futuristic security control room:
1. Wide shot of a dark control room with a wall of glowing CCTV monitors.
2. Close-up of monitors showing a city street, a shopping mall and an office lobby.
3. Operator's face lit by screen glow, calm and alert.
4. Hands moving over a keyboard and a touch panel with teal interface graphics (no readable text).
5. A teal highlight box locks onto a person on one screen.
6. Orbiting wide shot of the operator surrounded by screens, a sense of control and safety.

## Scene 5: Protection in Action (0:40–0:50)
[Storyboard Style Anchor] Six sequential panels showing different places being protected:
1. Officer greeting employees in a bright corporate lobby.
2. Close-up of a smiling employee nodding thanks.
3. Officer guiding a busy crowd at an outdoor evening event with string lights.
4. Patrol vehicle with hazard lights parked at a gated residential community at dusk.
5. Officer opening the gate for a family walking in.
6. Wide warm-lit shot of the officer standing watch as the neighborhood glows peacefully.

## Scene 6: The Promise & Call to Action (0:50–1:00)
[Storyboard Style Anchor] Six sequential panels of a triumphant finale:
1. Wide aerial drone shot above a calm city at golden hour.
2. Drone descends toward a rooftop where a team of officers stands in a line.
3. Side view of the team standing together, facing the horizon, flags and wind in motion.
4. Close-up of the lead officer's determined face, golden rim light.
5. Camera pulls back and upward, the city glowing beneath.
6. Final frame: a clean deep-navy background with soft golden light rays and ample empty space in the center for a logo.

## Optional: single-frame versions
If Flow generates grids poorly, run each line item as its own 16:9 image prompt: `[Look: cinematic, deep navy and teal grade, gold highlights, same officer as above] + <panel description>`. Then arrange the 6 images per scene in your editor.
