# Atlantis Security Agency: 60-Second Promo (6 × 10s Flow prompts)

**Tone:** Cinematic, premium, trustworthy. **Palette:** deep navy, ocean teal, gold accents. **Aspect:** 16:9.
**Tip:** Paste the "Style Anchor" at the start of every prompt so Flow keeps the look consistent across clips. Edit the voiceover (VO) lines and add your logo, contact info and tagline in post-production (Flow doesn't render text reliably).

**Style Anchor:**
> Cinematic 4K, shallow depth of field, deep navy and teal color grade with subtle gold highlights, dramatic soft lighting, smooth camera movement, professional corporate security aesthetic, no on-screen text.

---

## Scene 1 (0:00–0:10): The Threat
**Prompt:** [Style Anchor] Night-time city skyline, rain-slicked streets. A dark office building lobby with flickering lights; a shadowy figure's silhouette lingers near a glass door. Slow push-in, tense atmosphere, low-key lighting.
**VO:** "Every day, threats move quietly through the dark. Are you ready?"
**SFX/Music:** Low drone, distant thunder, heartbeat pulse.

## Scene 2 (0:10–0:20): The Guardian Arrives
**Prompt:** [Style Anchor] Sunrise breaks over the same city; golden light floods the street. A professional security officer in a sharp navy uniform walks confidently toward the camera, stopping to look straight at the lens. Low-angle hero shot, slow motion.
**VO:** "Meet Atlantis Security Agency. Protection you can trust, around the clock."
**SFX/Music:** Music swells to an uplifting orchestral theme.

## Scene 3 (0:20–0:30): Trained Professionals
**Prompt:** [Style Anchor] Bright modern training facility. A team of uniformed security guards in a disciplined briefing, an instructor pointing at a tactical map, then a close-up of focused, confident faces. Smooth lateral tracking shot.
**VO:** "Our licensed, rigorously trained officers are ready for anything."
**SFX/Music:** Rhythmic percussion enters.

## Scene 4 (0:30–0:40): Technology & Surveillance
**Prompt:** [Style Anchor] Sleek control room with large CCTV monitors showing a city, a mall and an office. An operator calmly monitors the screens as a glowing teal interface highlights a key point. Slow orbiting camera, futuristic ambiance.
**VO:** "Backed by 24/7 monitoring and smart surveillance technology."
**SFX/Music:** Soft electronic pulses layered over the theme.

## Scene 5 (0:40–0:50): Protection in Action
**Prompt:** [Style Anchor] Split-moment montage in one continuous feel: a guard greeting employees at a corporate lobby, an officer guiding crowds at a busy event, a patrol vehicle with hazard lights at a gated residential community at dusk. Warm, reassuring lighting, dynamic cuts.
**VO:** "From businesses and events to homes and communities, we keep what matters safe."
**SFX/Music:** Music builds toward the climax.

## Scene 6 (0:50–1:00): The Promise & Call to Action
**Prompt:** [Style Anchor] Wide aerial drone shot rising over a calm, glowing city at golden hour, with a team of Atlantis security officers standing together in the foreground, facing the horizon. Camera pulls back and fades to a clean navy background with soft light rays (space left for the logo).
**VO:** "Atlantis Security Agency. Safety, Integrity, Vigilance. Contact us today."
**SFX/Music:** Triumphant finish, then a soft fade.
**Post-production:** Add logo, tagline, phone, website and email over the final 3 seconds.

## Flow Tips
- Use Flow's "Extend"/frame-to-video: end each clip on the first frame of the next for smoother continuity.
- Keep the same officer description ("navy uniform, gold badge, short dark hair") in scenes 2, 3, 5 and 6 for consistency.
- Generate the voiceover separately (e.g. TTS or a real voice) and sync in an editor, since each clip's audio may vary.
